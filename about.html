ABOUT

I am Amaan Siddiqui, a 6th semester B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student specializing in Cloud Computing at Lovely Professional University. I have a strong interest in full-stack web development and am currently building my skills in the MERN stack, with a focus on creating scalable, efficient, and user-friendly applications.

I enjoy exploring the depths of computers and technology, constantly learning how systems work and how software can be used to solve real-world problems. Through hands-on learning, I have worked on projects such as MERN-based applications and practical development tasks that have helped me strengthen my understanding of modern web technologies.

I am passionate about problem-solving, continuous improvement, and applying my knowledge to build meaningful digital solutions. My career goal is to grow into a skilled full-stack developer and contribute to innovative and impactful projects.

Beyond academics and technology, I actively engage in sports such as football and running, which help me develop discipline, teamwork, consistency, and maintain a balanced lifestyle.
